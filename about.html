Since Day One

Fewer, better things.

Gentify Essentials started with a simple frustration: most men's accessories are either mass-produced and forgettable, or priced for a lifestyle most of us aren't living. We wanted a middle ground — pieces built from real materials, priced fairly, and designed to be worn for years rather than one season.

Every cap, watch, fragrance, tee and wallet in our catalogue is chosen (or made) with the same question in mind: would we wear this every day, ourselves? If the answer isn't a clear yes, it doesn't make the cut.
